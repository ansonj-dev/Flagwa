## What the repository licence covers

The Apache-2.0 grant applies to original FLAGWA source code, project-authored documentation and original project materials for which the relevant copyright holder has authority to license them, unless a file or component carries a separate licence or notice.

The licence does not automatically cover or relicense:
- NASA/ISRO/NISAR data products or source imagery;
- contextual datasets such as JRC Global Surface Water, ESA WorldCover, Copernicus DEM, GPM IMERG, WorldPop or OpenStreetMap;
- externally hosted Esri basemap tiles or other web services;
- the bundled NASA PIA18033 illustration, which follows NASA media-use guidance and is credited separately;
- third-party libraries, fonts, icons and other packages listed in the frontend and Python dependency manifests;
- trademarks, logos, names or insignia owned by NASA, ISRO, Space Apps, Esri or other parties.

Each third-party item retains its own applicable licence, attribution and service terms. The root LICENSE must not be read as granting rights in those materials.

## NASA Space Apps source and licence documentation

The 2026 participant terms require participants to ensure that third-party solution materials are freely and publicly available without restriction or, at minimum, are open source under the OSI definition. Any licences applicable to solution materials must be identified in the submitted documentation, and every source used—including free and open-source resources—must be cited.

The [Third-party Notices and Source Register](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) records currently identified assets, data-source categories and software manifests. Update that register for the exact data, tools, imagery and assets actually used in the submission. Generate and review a complete transitive package licence report before claiming that every dependency has been individually audited.
